Handmade Candle Safety Protocol

To prevent fire and physical injury, you must strictly follow our burning safety parameters:

• Do NOT burn your candle for longer than 4 hours at any single session.
• ALWAYS burn your candle within an active line of sight. Never leave an open flame unattended.
• Keep away from children, family pets, and flammable objects.
• Burn strictly on a flat, stable, level, and heat-resistant surface structure.
• Trim the cotton wick to 1/4 inch before lighting to ensure a clean, controlled burn.

True Soap Application Safety

Our hand-poured soaps are traditional cleansing bars. Review safe application rules:

• Intended for washing and body cleansing utility only.
• If irritation, redness, or discomfort occurs, discontinue use immediately.
• Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, flush thoroughly with clean water.
• Not for ingestion. Do not swallow or consume.
